# Fish Audio — 30-second launch video

**Company:** Fish Audio is studio-grade AI text-to-speech and voice cloning: type a script, tag the emotion, and get a voice that actually performs it. Launching **Drama 3**, its most expressive, emotionally controllable real-time voice model.

**Audience:** creators (YouTube, audiobooks, games, animation) and developers building voice agents.

**Goal:** launch video for Product Hunt, the website hero and paid social.

**One message:** Voice AI that doesn't sound like an AI voice.

**Must show:**
1. The hook: a flat, robotic waveform. "Most AI voices sound exactly like this."
2. The problem: regenerating the same line over and over and still getting zero emotion.
3. The product: the Fish Audio text-to-speech editor. Emotion tags (`[whispering]`, `[sobbing]`, `[laughing]`) go into the script, Generate is clicked, and the waveform comes alive.
4. The payoff: 2,000,000+ voices, clone any voice in 15 s, 30+ languages, 8M+ creators.
5. Logo + CTA: Fish Audio, "The most expressive AI speech", "Try it free · fish.audio".

**Tone:** confident, clean, fast. Premium SaaS launch, not cartoonish. No voiceover, music + SFX only.

**Deliverables:** 30 s master in 16:9 (1920 × 1080, 60 fps), plus 1:1 and 9:16 cutdowns.

**Assets:** Fish Audio logo (fish built from waveform bars, from fish.audio), brand palette (warm black `#0f0e0d`, cream `#f4eeeb`, warm greys `#766f6a` / `#a6a09b`, teal `#0089a8`), fonts Onest (headings/UI) and Geist Mono (tags). The editor UI is rebuilt as vector DOM from the live fish.audio text-to-speech panel, so it stays sharp under camera zooms.
