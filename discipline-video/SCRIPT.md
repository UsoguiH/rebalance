# Narration script: "The Line"

Paste each block into ElevenLabs on its own (Multilingual v2) and save it as the file
name shown. The `<break>` tags are real pauses. Leave them in.

---

### s01.mp3: The Pen
```
Every single day, you write one line of your story. <break time="0.8s" /> Whether you mean to... or not. <break time="1.2s" /> Most people never notice they're holding the pen.
```

### s02.mp3: The Lie of Motivation
```
We wait to feel ready. <break time="0.6s" /> We wait for motivation, like it's a train that's going to pull up and carry us somewhere better. <break time="0.8s" /> But motivation is weather. <break time="0.5s" /> It shows up when it wants... and it leaves without saying goodbye.
```

### s03.mp3: What Discipline Is
```
Discipline isn't punishment. <break time="0.5s" /> It isn't being hard on yourself. <break time="0.9s" /> Discipline is keeping a promise... to the one person you can never hide from. <break time="1.0s" /> You.
```

### s04.mp3: Small & Boring
```
And it's never one big moment. <break time="0.6s" /> It's small. <break time="0.4s" /> It's boring. <break time="0.5s" /> It's ten pages, not the whole book. <break time="0.3s" /> One set, not the whole body. <break time="0.3s" /> One honest hour, not a perfect life. <break time="0.9s" /> One line. <break time="0.5s" /> Then another.
```

### s05.mp3: The Shaky Line
```
Here's the hard part. <break time="0.8s" /> Some days, the line will be ugly. <break time="0.5s" /> Shaky. <break time="0.5s" /> Barely there. <break time="1.0s" /> Write it anyway. <break time="0.8s" /> Because a shaky line still counts. <break time="0.4s" /> A blank page never does.
```

### s06.mp3: Nobody Watching
```
Nobody claps at five a.m. <break time="0.5s" /> Nobody sees the rep you almost skipped... or the night you said no, when yes was easier. <break time="1.0s" /> Good. <break time="0.6s" /> That's where it's built. In the rooms nobody's watching. <break time="0.8s" /> Every promise you keep there becomes proof. <break time="0.5s" /> And proof... is louder than any excuse.
```

### s07.mp3: The Staircase
```
And then one day, you look back... <break time="0.8s" /> and all those small, boring, shaky lines... <break time="0.6s" /> have become a staircase. <break time="1.2s" /> You didn't find a new life. <break time="0.5s" /> You wrote one. <break time="0.4s" /> Line by line.
```

### s08.mp3: Pick Up the Pen
```
So don't wait to feel ready. <break time="0.6s" /> You won't. <break time="1.0s" /> Just pick up the pen... <break time="0.8s" /> and write today's line.
```
