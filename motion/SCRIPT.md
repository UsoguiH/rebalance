# Voice-over script (ElevenLabs)

Paste the block below into ElevenLabs as one generation. The original read is

between sentences. Don't add music; export MP3 or WAV.

```
When you know nobody at an event, talk to this person first.
Skip the speaker and the CEO.
Find the connector. The guy who knows half the room.
Say his name's Daniel. You'll spot him fast.
He never stays in one group. And he's always the one who waves the new guy over.
Tonight, that's you.

You're thinking, why would a guy like that help me?
```
