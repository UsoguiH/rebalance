# Typing clicks

`key01.wav` – `key12.wav` are single keystrokes cut from the typewriter captions of a launch video the project owner supplied as the reference sound, recorded 2026-10-10. Each one is mono, 44.1 kHz, about 40–70 ms long: a 90 Hz high-pass to drop rumble, a 1 ms fade-in from 2 ms before the attack, a 30 ms fade-out, and normalized to −1 dBFS.

Use them for every typed line in these motion pieces: `typing-sfx.js` loads them in a browser, and `motion/teaser/build.py` mixes them offline.
