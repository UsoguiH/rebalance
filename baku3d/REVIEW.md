## Style (src/style.js)

1. Red is too saturated and flat: pure #ff1a1a curtains with vertical bands. The reference backdrop is a deeper crimson (#a0182c-#c0283a) with a warm orange-gold glow at the top right and a dark vignette. Re-grade: lower the red saturation, add a gradient glow (light from the upper-right) and a darker lower area behind the legs.
2. Hatching is uniform, wavy and everywhere (blue-lavender lines across all white cloth, lit sides included). The reference uses crisp, short directional ink strokes only in shadow areas and a few folds. It has big areas of clean pure white on the lit sides and solid spot-black fills in the deepest creases (under the lapel, between the legs, inside the boot). Reduce hatch density on lit areas, make the strokes straighter and follow the form, and add a solid black threshold for the darkest values.
3. Outlines are one thick uniform weight. The reference uses a thin line for details and thick on silhouettes. Vary the line width by depth/crease, and thin the interior lines (face and hands) to ~1px at this frame size.
4. Face shading is dirty: hatching over the jaw/mouth turns into a dark smear, and the skin has no soft gradient. Disable or reduce hatching on the skin role in the face; use two-tone cel shading with a warm orange mid-shadow, plus a light halftone on the shadow side only.
5. Stray noise. There is dotted speckle near the left hand and boot (~(280-330,960-1010)), stipple artefacts along the right edge (1130-1156, 760-1100), and scribble marks at the right edge. Clip them, and avoid hatching of the backdrop at the screen border.
6. Wall. Thick brown mortar lines on a flat cream wall. The reference wall is pale cream/white with a warm glow (orange tint near the throne), very fine grey mortar lines and grunge, with the bricks barely visible. Thin the mortar lines to about 40% opacity and add a subtle vignette.
7. Colour grade and mood. The render looks like a bright flat cartoon. The reference is warmer and more contrasty and "menacing-lazy": a golden rim light from the upper right, heavy shadows on his left side, and a slight desaturated paper look in the whites (cool grey-white, not lavender). Add a warm key from the right, a stronger shadow term, and swap the lavender shadow tone for a neutral cool grey.
8. Throne rendering (shared with A): throne gold needs less flat orange and more warm yellow-brown gradient with a dark ink line. See area (5) in the notes below.
