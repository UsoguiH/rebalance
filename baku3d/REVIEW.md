## Style (src/style.js)

1. **Suit whites are flat and creamy.** The trousers and sleeves are large, clean, yellow-cream areas with little ink. The reference whites are cool grey-white with sharp black ink-hatched folds, spot-black creases (under the lapel, between the legs, inside the boot top) and crisp edges. Cool the whites (#f4f4f6), and add a hard black threshold for the deepest 10% of tones, so that shadow creases turn solid black, as in the reference.
2. **Skin shading is blotchy.** Pink/orange blotches (cheeks, forehead, neck) read as a dirty airbrush. Replace them with two-tone cel shading (lit/shadow) with a crisp terminator, and a fine hatching band on the shadow side only. Keep the stubble hatching away from the mouth.
3. **Backdrop glow.** The orange diagonal spotlight beam at (600-900,0-300) is too hard-edged and bright. The reference glow is a soft warm gradient at the top right, over deep crimson (#a0182c to #c0283a), darker toward the bottom. Soften the beam edge, lower its saturation, and darken the lower red.
4. **Ink lines.** Silhouette outlines are consistent and thick, which suits the panel, but the interior lines (face, hands, fingers) are the same weight and too heavy, so the hand fingers look outlined with a marker. Thin the interior lines to ~1-1.5px, and keep thick lines for the silhouette and folds in shadow.
5. **Hatching is now too sparse on the body.** The lit side is clean, but the shadow areas of the jacket (e.g. under the right arm (780-1000,560-690) and by the left shoulder (160-330,560-700)) only have faint lines. The reference has dense, short, directional strokes in the shadows. Increase the density in the shadow bands and add cross-hatching in the darkest areas.
6. **Boot and throne material.** The boot is a smooth brown with a tan highlight patch, and the gold armrests are smooth orange-yellow gradients. The reference leather is almost black in shadow with a few sharp orange highlights, and the throne gold is brown-gold with carved darkness. Add darker values and a stronger specular, with a harder cel step.
7. **Stray artifacts.** A black curved scribble at the right edge (1055-1156,765-870), a dark smudge at (1100-1156,1170-1220), and a soft floor shadow patch at (660-900,1030-1100) look wrong. Clip or remove them. The wall mortar lines are acceptable now, but add a slight vignette at the edges.
8. **Colour grade and mood.** The overall look is brighter and more cheerful than the reference. The reference is warm, golden, high-contrast and "lazy menacing", with heavy shadow around the face (left side) and under the brow. Add a subtle darkening vignette and stronger shadow under the brow/jaw, and push the key light warmer from the upper right.
