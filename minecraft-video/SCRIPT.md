# Steve vs Herobrine: narration script

Hand-drawn story video in Look 1 (notebook + blue ballpoint) and Look 4 (whiteboard + marker),
rendered from the same voice track. Paste the block below into ElevenLabs (Multilingual v2).

```
Every Minecraft world has a rule nobody tells you. <break time="0.6s" /> Don't stay out after dark... <break time="0.5s" /> because someone is always watching. <break time="1.5s" />

Day one. <break time="0.5s" /> Steve punched his first tree, built a tiny wooden house, and placed one torch by the door. <break time="0.6s" /> Life was simple. <break time="1.5s" />

But on the third night, things started to change. <break time="0.6s" /> Trees lost their leaves. <break time="0.4s" /> Tunnels appeared that Steve never dug. <break time="0.6s" /> And at the edge of the fog... <break time="0.8s" /> two white eyes. <break time="1.5s" />

Herobrine. <break time="1.0s" /> The ghost in the code. <break time="0.5s" /> No sound. <break time="0.3s" /> No shadow. <break time="0.3s" /> Just watching. <break time="1.5s" />

Steve ran. <break time="0.4s" /> He blocked the door. He dug down. He hid in the dark... <break time="0.6s" /> but every time he turned around, Herobrine was closer. <break time="1.5s" />

So Steve stopped running. <break time="0.6s" /> He mined iron. <break time="0.3s" /> He crafted a sword. <break time="0.4s" /> And he lit up every corner of his world... <break time="0.4s" /> one torch at a time. <break time="1.5s" />

When Herobrine came back, the night was full of light. <break time="0.8s" /> And for the first time... <break time="0.6s" /> Herobrine took a step back. <break time="1.5s" />

Steve didn't win because he was stronger. <break time="0.5s" /> He won because he was ready. <break time="1.0s" /> Darkness only wins where you stop placing torches. <break time="1.2s" />

So... <break time="0.5s" /> how many torches did you place today?
```

## Emotional version (Eleven v3, audio tags)

Use the **Eleven v3** model for this one. It reads the [tags] as acting directions and
does not speak them. v3 ignores `<break>` tags, so pauses come from "...", "—" and line breaks.

```
[whispers] Okay... every Minecraft world has a rule nobody tells you.
[pause] Don't stay out after dark.
[nervous] Because someone... is always watching.

[cheerful] Day one! Steve punches his first tree, builds this tiny little wooden house — [chuckles] honestly, kind of ugly — and puts ONE torch by the door.
[content sigh] Life was simple.

[serious] But on the third night... things started to change.
[curious] The trees... lost their leaves.
[uneasy] Tunnels showed up that Steve never dug.
[whispers] And at the edge of the fog...
[gasps] two. white. eyes.

[slowly, quietly] Herobrine.
[ominous] The ghost in the code.
No sound. No shadow. [whispers] Just... watching.

[panicked, fast] So Steve ran! He blocked the door — he dug straight down — he hid in the dark —
[breathless] but every time he turned around...
[terrified whisper] Herobrine was closer.

[long pause] [sighs] And then... Steve stopped running.
[determined] He mined iron.
He crafted a sword.
[building excitement] And he lit up every corner of his world... one torch... at a time.

[confident] So when Herobrine came back — the night was FULL of light.
[amazed] And for the first time ever...
[whispers, smiling] Herobrine took a step back.

[warm, thoughtful] See — Steve didn't win because he was stronger.
He won because he was ready.
[softly] Darkness only wins where you stop placing torches.

[playful] So... [laughs softly] how many torches did YOU place today?
```
