# ANIMATION.md: motion spec

All motion comes from **real spring physics**. `public/js/motion.js` simulates `x'' = (−k(x−1) − c·v) / m` and samples the result into a CSS `linear()` easing, so the same springs work in CSS transitions (`--spring-*` / `--dur-*` in `springs.css`, generated by `npm run springs`) and in Web Animations (`spring(el, keyframes, name)`). There is no animation library and no React; the behaviour mirrors the two Framer Motion references.

| Spring | stiffness / damping / mass | Feel | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `smooth` | 200 / 22 / 1.2 | Soft, slight overshoot | Expand/collapse height and width (**ExpandDetails** reference) |
| `snappy` | 400 / 30 / 0.8 | Quick, settled | Shared-element morph (**ViewOnMap** reference), segmented thumb, menus |
| `gentle` | 246.7 / 31.4 / 1 (≈ duration .4s, bounce 0) | No overshoot | Content blur-in/out, page reveals |
| `bouncy` | 380 / 17 / 1 | Playful | Press release, check marks, badges, number ticks, dots |
| `sheet` | 300 / 32 / 1 | Weighty | Bottom sheet, nav indicator, envelope doors |

## Reference 1: ExpandDetails → `.xp[data-morph]` (`m3.js › initExpandables`)
- **Open:** width springs first (delay 0), height follows (delay **.25s**), and the content enters with `opacity 0 · blur(8px) · y 40 → rest` (delay **.3s**, gentle).
- **Close:** content exits `blur(8px) · y 16`, height collapses first, then width (delay **.3s**).
- The chevron rotates −90° (mirrored in RTL), easing out over .2s with a .3s delay when opening.
- Used for «تفاصيل الباقة» on Home and «التفاصيل» on the dashboard. A height-only variant drives the FAQ items, the design groups, and the builder's switch-revealed fields.

## Reference 2: ViewOnMap → guest page (`guest.js › toggleMap`)
- The pill «عرض على الخريطة» (180–220×56, radius 28) **becomes** the map card (square, radius 32). Instead of a scale transform, which would distort the content, the card is laid out at its final size and its `clip-path: inset(… round r)` springs from the pill's rectangle to the full card. The container height springs alongside. Spring: **snappy 400/30/0.8**.
- The map fades in after .15s with the reference's muted filter (`grayscale(.9) sepia(.1) contrast(1.05)`). The M3 loading indicator covers it until the iframe loads.
- The close button pops in (`scale .5 → 1`, bouncy). Closing reverses the clip back into the pill, which then settles in with `scale .9 → 1`.

## Apple-style micro-interactions
- **Press:** every `.pressable` shrinks to `scale(.96)` in 120ms on `:active` and releases on the **bouncy** spring (so it overshoots slightly on the way back). A Material state layer (6–10% overlay) sits under it.
- **Button → loading morph:** a pressed submit button's label fades out, the pill springs into a circle, and the morphing loading indicator appears (`setLoading`).
- **Shake on error:** an invalid field gets a damped −8px/+6px shake plus a snackbar.
- **Number ticks:** when stats change, the number swaps and pops (`scale 1.35 → 1`, bouncy). Countdown digits blur in.
- **Stepper:** the value slides up or down depending on direction, and hitting a limit gives a small horizontal "no" shake.
- **Bottom sheet:** opens on the sheet spring, can be dragged down to dismiss (>110px), rubber-bands when dragged upward (`−√dy·2`), and snaps back on the bouncy spring.
- **Nav indicator** glides between items on the sheet spring. Page **dots** stretch from 8px to 24px on bouncy.
- **Scroll reveal:** sections blur in (`y 28`) with a 60ms stagger.
- **Hero depth:** the product stage parallaxes at 0.18× scroll and the calligraphy fades as you scroll.
- **Page transitions:** cross-document View Transitions (old page fades and blurs out, new page blurs in on the gentle spring).
- **Design picker:** the selected design gets an orange ring, and its check badge pops in on the bouncy spring.

All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (springs collapse to 1ms and reveals are skipped).
